I have a background in cultural geography with a particular interest in socio-ecological relations, visual methods, and ethnographies.

In London, I'm a member of a photography collective using situationist methods to explore connections with place. We have exhibited at Filet Gallery, sold magazines at the ICA, and have copies housed within the V&A library.

I am the Digital & Communications Manager for the Society of Architectural Historians of Great Britain. I write website content, newsletters, and work to develop the digital offering.
