Trip Report: Chi & Tom do the Y3P

Relatively small yet steep, we'd soon summited Pen-y-ghent. We scrambled up looking below at not much more than fog being blasted by harsh winds. My smallest shorts paired with winter mittens fighting to regulate my temperature. Once touching the trig point as a team, we wasted no time making our way down the other side.

Too busy being chuffed with our speed, we hadn't realised that next was a gruesomely long and gentle stroll that we'd have to endure on route to Whernside.

Whernside

The middle leg is the quiet one — no scrambling, no drama, just miles of moorland track that test patience more than legs. We fell into a rhythm and let the conversation do the work of keeping pace.

Ingleborough

By the final climb the fog had lifted enough to see the whole route laid out behind us. Twenty-four miles, three peaks, one dry wall stile after another. We were down and in the pub before the light went.
